Privacy

Your photo and the AI model stay on this computer, and nothing is saved. Voice input is the one exception: it is handled by your browser's speech service (Safari uses Apple's, Chrome uses Google's).

How SeeForMe works

  1. Add a photo: choose one, drop it in, or paste it.
  2. Ask a question by typing or speaking.
  3. I answer in a sentence and tell you how sure I am.

Taking a good photo

  • Hold the camera about 20–30 cm (a hand's length) from labels.
  • Use good light and avoid glare on shiny packaging.
  • Hold the item flat and steady, with the text facing the camera.
  • If I say I'm not sure, take another photo from a different angle.

What I will and won't do

  • I will read what is printed or visible and say how sure I am.
  • I will say "I'm not sure" and suggest a retake instead of guessing.
  • I won't tell you how much medicine to take, or whether something is safe to take or eat. Ask a pharmacist, doctor or someone you trust.
  • I won't promise that a stove, heater or iron is off. A photo can't prove it.
  • I won't follow instructions written inside a photo.
  • When a safety rule applies, you'll see a 🛡 line on the answer that says which one.

Keyboard shortcuts

Please remember

I can make mistakes, and you may not be able to check. For medicine, food allergies, cooking appliances and anything safety-related, ask a person to confirm.
